# Working as a team

Several people can work from the same mailbox, such as `support@yourcompany.com` or `sales@yourcompany.com`. Nobody has to share the mailbox password.

## Owners and members

Every connected mailbox has one **owner** and any number of **members**.

- **The owner** is the person who connected the mailbox. They decide who has access and how the AI behaves on it.
- **Members** are people the owner invited. They work the email day to day.

You can see your role on **Settings → Accounts**: a mailbox someone else shared with you has a **Shared with you** label.

## Who can do what

| What | Owner | Member |
|---|---|---|
| Read every email in the mailbox | ✅ | ✅ |
| Reply, send, and use AI Reply, Rewrite and Summarize | ✅ | ✅ |
| Add internal notes | ✅ | ✅ |
| Assign an email to someone, snooze it | ✅ | ✅ |
| Ask questions about the mailbox (**Ask**) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Add and edit contacts | ✅ | ✅ |
| See the member list, writing style, Insights and Suggestions | ✅ | ✅ |
| Approve or reject an AI reply when named as an approver | ✅ | ✅ |
| Invite or remove people | ✅ | ❌ |
| Change what the AI does: rules, automatic replies, agents, blocked phrases | ✅ | ❌ |
| Change the writing style, approve Suggestions | ✅ | ❌ |
| Connect Slack, Teams or HubSpot | ✅ | ❌ |
| Pause syncing, change sync filters, disconnect the mailbox | ✅ | ❌ |

If a button mentioned in this Help isn't on your screen, you're probably a member, and it's an owner-only button. Ask the mailbox's owner.

## Inviting someone (owner)

1. Ask the person to **sign up for the app first**, with the email address they'll use. You can't invite someone who doesn't have an account yet.
2. Go to **Settings → Accounts** and click **Members** on the mailbox.
3. Type their email address and click **Invite**.

They now see the mailbox in their Inbox straight away. Nothing is emailed to them, so tell them yourself.

If you see *"No user with that email has an account here yet"*, they haven't signed up, or they used a different address.

## Dividing the work

- **Assign** an email so everyone knows who's handling it: open the email and pick a name from the dropdown at the top (it shows **Unassigned** until someone is chosen). It appears once a mailbox has two or more people. On a phone the dropdown is hidden, so assign from a computer.
- **Leave a note** with **Notes** at the top of an email: *"Called them, they prefer Tuesday."* Notes are **never sent** to the customer. Only people with access to the mailbox see them.
- **Check who has what** on the **Insights** page, under **Open work per person**.

## Removing someone (owner)

When someone leaves the team or no longer needs access:

1. Go to **Settings → Accounts** and click **Members** on the mailbox.
2. Click **Remove** next to their name.

**What happens straight away:**
- They can no longer open the mailbox or any of its emails.
- Emails assigned to them become **Unassigned**, so pick someone new on anything still open.
- Any AI reply that was waiting for **their** approval is cancelled. The draft goes back to **Drafts**, where you can check it and send it yourself.
- Notes they wrote stay on the emails.

Members can't remove themselves. If you want to leave a shared mailbox, ask its owner.

The owner can't be removed. To stop the whole team using a mailbox, the owner disconnects it, which removes it for everyone.

## Good habits for teams

- **Remove people the day they leave.** Don't wait for a clean-up later.
- **Assign before you start writing**, so two people don't answer the same customer.
- **Write notes for the next person**, not for yourself: what you did, and what's still open.
- **Use approvals for anything sensitive** such as pricing, refunds or legal topics. The owner sets these up in a rule; see [Approval Chains](/help/07-settings-accounts#approval-chains).
- **Keep the AI's rules simple** and tell the team what they do, so nobody is surprised by an automatic reply.
