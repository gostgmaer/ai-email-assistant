# Insights

## What it is

One page showing how an inbox is doing: how fast people get a first response, who has open work, what the AI did, and what stopped it from auto-sending.

## Where to find it

**Insights** in the left sidebar. If you have more than one account, choose it at the top of the page. Any member of the account can see it.

## How to use it

The numbers are worked out overnight, and the top of the page says when they were last updated. Click **Update now** to work them out straight away. A new account shows *"Not computed yet"* until then.

## What each part means

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Last 7 days / The 7 days before** | The median time to a first response, plus how many threads were answered and how many weren't yet. Each thread counts in the week its first email arrived. |
| **Open work per person** | Inbox threads per assignee: **Needs a reply**, and **Waiting on someone else** (you sent the last message). |
| **AI replies per week** | The last 4 weeks: **Auto-sent**, **Held, then sent**, **Held, then discarded**, and how much drafts were edited on average before sending. |
| **AI replies per agent** | The same counts for each AI agent over the last 30 days. **Default assistant** means no agent wrote it. |
| **What held replies back** | Which safety check stopped a reply that a rule wanted to auto-send, over the last 30 days. |

## Example

> **Last 7 days:** 1.1 h median first response · 12 answered, 3 not yet
>
> **The 7 days before:** 4.0 h median first response · 15 answered, 1 not yet

Responses got faster this week, but 3 threads from this week still have no answer. Check **Open work per person** to see who has them.

## What counts

- **Response times** leave out newsletters and notifications, which don't expect a reply. An auto-sent AI reply counts as a response.
- **Average edit:** 0% means sent as the AI wrote it; 100% means completely rewritten.
- **Safety checks** cover the urgent-message check, personal data, your prohibited phrases, claims that something was done or attached, replies grounded in your documents, the AI's own review, and send limits.

## What happens

Looking at this page changes nothing and sends nothing. Disconnecting an account deletes its insights.
