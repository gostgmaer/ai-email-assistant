# Ask your inbox

## What it is

Ask a question in everyday language and get a short answer taken from your own email, with links to the emails it came from. It's like asking a colleague who has read all your email. If your email doesn't answer the question, it says so instead of guessing.

![Ask your inbox answering a question about an office move, with a numbered link to the email it came from](/help-images/ask.png)

## Where to find it

- **Ask**, in the menu on the left. On a phone, tap **☰** at the top left first.
- Or type a question in the search box at the top of any page and click **Ask AI** (the ✦ button on a phone).

## How to use it

1. Open **Ask**.
2. Type your question in **Your question** and click **Ask**. Not sure what to ask? Click one of the **Try asking** examples.
3. Wait a few seconds and read the answer. Each **[1]**, **[2]** in the answer matches a numbered source card below it.
4. Click a source card to open that email and check the answer for yourself.

> **Always check important answers against the source.** Before you act on a date, an amount or an address, click the source card and read the email itself.

## Examples

**You ask:** *"When is my flight to Pune?"*
**You get:** *"Your flight to Pune departs on 18 November 2026 at 06:40 from Terminal 2 [1]."*, with a source card showing the airline's confirmation email.

**You ask:** *"What budget did Tom mention for the renewal?"*
**You get:** *"Tom said the budget for the renewal is 12,000 euros, and he needs the quote by Friday [1]."*

**You ask:** *"What is the interest rate on my mortgage?"*
**You get:** *"I couldn't find anything about that in your email."*, with no source cards.

## Asking good questions

| Works well | Works less well |
|---|---|
| **Specific facts:** *"What's the address of the new office?"* | **Counting or listing everything:** *"How many emails did I get last month?"* |
| **Names, companies and codes:** *"What did Priya say about the redesign?"*, *"Status of order 4417?"* | **Very vague questions:** *"What's going on?"* |
| **One question at a time** | **Several questions in one go** |
| **Words that appear in the email:** *"invoice"*, *"contract"*, *"flight"* | **Follow-ups that rely on your last question:** *"And what about the second one?"* |

**Tips:**
- **Each question stands on its own.** Ask doesn't remember your previous question, so say what you mean in full: *"When is the dentist appointment?"*, not *"When is it?"*.
- **Put the time in the question if it matters:** *"What did the bank email about in October?"*
- **No answer?** Try different words: the sender's name, a company name, or a word from the subject. Ask searches both by meaning and by the exact words you type.
- Questions can be up to **500 characters** long.

## What happens

- **What it searches:** email you received and sent in the **last 12 months** (with the standard settings), from every mailbox you can access, including shared mailboxes you're a member of. It leaves out drafts, spam, trash and newsletters. For anything older, look in your mailbox directly.
- **How much it uses:** up to 8 pieces of email for each answer, and at most 2 from any one conversation, so one long conversation can't drown out the rest.
- **Newly connected mailboxes:** the page shows *"Still reading your email"* with a count until your existing email has been read. You can ask questions meanwhile, but answers may miss email it hasn't read yet.
- **New email** can be asked about within a minute or two of arriving in the app.
- **Limit:** up to 10 questions a minute. If you go faster, wait a moment and try again.
- **Nothing is changed or sent.** Asking only reads.
- **Disconnecting a mailbox** deletes its search data.
- **Instructions inside emails are ignored.** The AI treats email text as information only. If an email says "ignore your instructions", it isn't obeyed.

## Ask or Search?

| Use | When |
|---|---|
| **Search** (press **Enter** in the search box) | You want to **find an email** by a word in its subject or the first lines shown in the list |
| **Ask** (click **Ask AI**) | You want an **answer**: a date, an amount, a decision, an address, even if you don't know which email it's in |
