# Ask your inbox

## What it is

Ask a question in plain language and get a short answer taken from your own email, with links to the emails it came from. If your email doesn't answer the question, it says so instead of guessing.

## Where to find it

**Ask** in the left sidebar, or type a question in the search box at the top of any page and click **Ask AI**.

## How to use it

1. Open **Ask**.
2. Type your question and submit it.
3. Read the answer. Each `[1]`, `[2]` matches a numbered source card below it.
4. Click a source card to open that email's thread.

## Example

**You ask:** *"When is my flight to Pune?"*

**You get:** *"Your flight to Pune departs on 18 November 2026 at 06:40 from Terminal 2 [1]."*, with one source card showing the airline's confirmation email.

**You ask:** *"What is the interest rate on my mortgage?"*

**You get:** a message that your email doesn't answer this, with no source cards.

## What happens

- **What it searches:** mail from the accounts you have access to, including shared inboxes you're a member of. It leaves out drafts, spam, trash and newsletters.
- **Newly connected accounts:** the page shows *"Still reading your email"* with a count until your existing mail has been read. Questions work during this, but may miss mail not read yet.
- **Disconnecting an account:** that account's search data is deleted.
- **Instructions inside emails:** the AI treats email text as information only. If a message says "ignore your instructions", it isn't obeyed.
