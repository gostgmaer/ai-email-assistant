# Settings → Security

## What it is

Your password, and a list of every device where you're signed in, so you can sign any of them out from wherever you are.

## Where to find it

**Settings → Security**, in the menu on the left. On a phone, tap **☰** at the top left first.

## Ways to sign in

- **Email and password:** the address and password you signed up with.
- **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft:** on the sign-in page, if you signed up that way. Google or Microsoft confirms who you are, and the app never sees that password.

You can have both: someone who signed up with Google can also set a password here, and then use either.

## Changing your password

**How to use it:**
1. Type your **Current password**. Leave it empty if you signed up with Google or Microsoft and have never set a password here.
2. Type a **New password** of at least 8 characters.
3. Click **Update password**.
4. A green *"Password updated."* message appears. If your current password was wrong, you'll see a red error and nothing changes.

**What happens:** for safety, changing your password signs you out on **every** device, including this one. With the standard settings, this happens within 15 minutes. Sign in again with the new password.

**Example:** you signed up with **Continue with Google** and never set a password. Leave **Current password** empty, type a new one and click **Update password**. From then on, you need the current password to change it.

> **A good password** is at least 8 characters, used nowhere else, and hard to guess. A short sentence such as `my cat naps at noon` is easy to remember and strong.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Type the email address you sign in with and send the request.
3. Open the email you receive and click its link within **1 hour**. If it doesn't arrive in a few minutes, check your spam folder.
4. Choose a new password.

Resetting your password also signs you out on every other device. If the link has expired, request a new one.

## Managing active sessions

**What it is:** a list of every phone, computer and browser where you're signed in. Each shows the device or browser, its internet address (IP address), and when you signed in. The one you're using now is marked **This device**.

**How to use it:**
- To sign out one other device: click **Revoke** next to it.
- To sign out everywhere at once, including this device: click **Sign out everywhere**.

**Example:** you see a sign-in from a device you don't recognise. Click **Revoke** on it straight away, then change your password above.

**What happens:** that device is signed out and has to sign in again with your password. With the standard settings, it can keep working for up to 15 minutes before it's locked out, so change your password straight away if you think someone else has it.

## Good habits

- **Use a password you don't use anywhere else**, of at least 8 characters. A short sentence is easy to remember and hard to guess.
- **Sign out on shared computers**: click your picture at the top right, then **Sign out**.
- **Look at Active sessions now and then**, and revoke anything you don't recognise.
- **Lost a device?** Click **Sign out everywhere**, sign in again on your own device, and change your password.
