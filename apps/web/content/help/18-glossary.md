# Glossary

Plain explanations of words you'll see in the app.

**Account, mailbox**
An email address connected to the app, such as your Gmail. You can connect several. Each one has its own settings.

**Primary**
The mailbox used by default when you write a new email.

**Sync**
Copying your email into the app. It happens automatically about every 5 minutes. **Sync now** does it immediately.

**Thread, conversation**
An email and all the replies to it, shown together.

**Needs reply, FYI, Notifications, Newsletters**
The groups the Inbox is sorted into. **Needs reply**: someone is waiting on you. **FYI**: worth reading, no answer expected. **Notifications**: automatic mail such as receipts. **Newsletters**: mailing lists.

**Draft**
An email that's written but not sent. AI drafts wait for you to check them.

**AI draft, AI reply**
A reply written by the AI. It's only sent when you click **Send**, unless the owner set up an automatic reply rule.

**Automatic reply, auto-send**
An AI reply that goes out without anyone reading it first. Off unless the mailbox's owner turns on a rule for it.

**Undo window**
A short wait before an automatic reply is sent, during which you can click **Cancel send**.

**Quiet hours**
Times when automatic replies wait instead of being sent, such as overnight.

**Policy, blocked phrases**
Words that stop an automatic reply from going out. A reply containing one waits as a draft for a person to check.

**Rule, workflow rule**
An instruction the owner sets up: *when an email like this arrives, do that*. For example: *when an email mentions "invoice", add the label Invoices*.

**Condition**
The "when" part of a rule, such as *subject contains "invoice"*.

**Action**
The "do that" part of a rule, such as *add a label* or *let the AI reply*.

**Agent, persona**
A set of instructions that gives the AI a particular voice or job, such as "Customer Support". A rule can tell the AI to reply as that agent.

**Writing style**
How you usually write: your greeting, sign-off and tone. The AI learns it from your sent email so its drafts sound like you.

**Grounded in your documents**
The AI used one of your uploaded documents for facts in this reply. Point at the label to see which.

**Calendar-aware**
The AI looked at how busy your calendar is before writing this reply. It never commits to a time on its own.

**Approval, approval chain**
One or more people who must sign off an AI reply, in order, before it's sent.

**Owner, member**
On a shared mailbox, the owner connected it and controls its settings. Members were invited to work on its email. See [Working as a team](/help/15-working-as-a-team).

**Assign**
Mark who on the team is handling an email.

**Internal note**
A comment on an email that only your team can see. It's never sent.

**Snooze**
Hide an email until later. It comes back to your Inbox at the time you chose.

**Task, You promised**
Things to do that the AI found in your email. **You promised** means something you said you'd do in an email you sent.

**Waiting**
Emails you sent that nobody has answered yet.

**Digest**
A once-a-day summary of unread emails and open tasks, at the hour you choose.

**Session**
A device or browser where you're signed in. You can sign any of them out under **Settings → Security**.

**IMAP, SMTP**
The standard way most email services let other apps read (IMAP) and send (SMTP) email. You only need these for mailboxes that aren't Gmail or Outlook. Your email provider or IT team can give you the settings.

**App password**
A special password some email providers make you create for apps like this one, instead of your normal password. Your provider's help pages explain how.

**Integration**
A connection to another service, such as Slack, Teams or HubSpot, so rules can post messages or save contacts there.

**Webhook**
An advanced option for technical teams: a rule sends a short notice to another system's web address. You can ignore it if you don't know you need it.
