# Safe use guidelines

The AI is a fast helper, not a replacement for your judgement. It writes well, but it can get facts wrong, and an email can't be called back once it's sent. These habits keep you in control.

## Before you send any AI-written email

Read it once, the way the person receiving it will. Check these five things:

1. **Facts and numbers.** Prices, amounts, dates, times, invoice numbers, quantities. The AI can mix these up or invent them.
2. **Names.** The person's name, your name, company names.
3. **Promises.** Did it promise something you can't or won't do, such as a refund, a discount, a deadline or "I've attached it"?
4. **Tone.** Is it right for this person? Too formal for a friend, too casual for a client?
5. **Recipients.** Is it going to the right people? Check **Cc** if you used **Reply all**.

If you see an amber **Check before sending** box on an AI draft, the AI itself thinks the draft may say things the conversation doesn't support. Read the listed points carefully and fix or remove them.

**There is no "unsend".** Once you click **Send**, the email has gone, as it would from Gmail or Outlook.

## When something's wrong with a draft

- **Small problems:** fix them in the reply box, then send.
- **Wrong from the start:** click **Discard** and pick a reason: **Wrong facts**, **Wrong tone**, **Shouldn't reply**, **Too long** or **Other**. It takes one click, and the reasons help the app suggest fixes on the [Suggestions](/help/13-suggestions) page.
- **Want different wording:** use **Rewrite** to make it shorter, friendlier, more formal and so on.

## Turning on automatic replies (owners)

By default the AI only writes drafts. A mailbox's owner can let it send some replies on its own. That saves time, but nobody reads those replies before they go out. If you decide to use it:

1. **Wait until you trust the drafts.** Use the app for a week or two first, and only automate replies you've been sending unchanged.
2. **Start narrow.** Automate one simple type of email, such as "thanks for your order" questions, not "every message". The starter rule *"Auto-reply — all messages"* is switched off on purpose.
3. **Add blocked phrases.** Under **Policy**, list words that must never go out without a person checking: for example `refund, discount, contract, legal, cancel, price`. A reply containing one is held as a draft instead.
4. **Give yourself time to stop it.** Under **Automation**, set an **Undo window** of 30 to 60 seconds, so each automatic reply waits and can be cancelled. Set **Quiet hours** so nothing goes out overnight.
5. **Keep the limits low** at first, such as 5 per hour. Over the limit, replies are held as drafts and you get a notification.
6. **Check the Activity log** every few days, under **Activity** on the mailbox, to see exactly what was sent and why.
7. **Use approvals** for sensitive replies, so one or more named people must sign off first. See [Approval Chains](/help/07-settings-accounts#approval-chains).

Not sure? Leave automatic replies off. Drafts alone already save most of the time.

## Protecting your account

- **Use a strong, unique password**, of at least 8 characters, that you don't use anywhere else. Change it under **Settings → Security**.
- **Sign out on shared or public computers**: click your picture at the top right, then **Sign out**.
- **Check your devices now and then** under **Settings → Security → Active sessions**. If you see one you don't recognise, click **Revoke** on it, which signs it out straight away, then change your password.
- **Lost a phone or laptop?** Click **Sign out everywhere**, then sign in again on your own device and change your password.
- **Don't put passwords, card numbers or ID numbers in emails**, and don't ask the AI to write them.

## What to share with the AI

- **Documents** you upload can be quoted in replies to anyone who writes to you. Only upload what you'd be happy for a customer to read: price lists, policies, product guides. Don't upload internal salary sheets, personal records or confidential contracts. See [Documents](/help/05-documents).
- **The AI only works on mail in the app.** It doesn't see your other files, your browser or other apps.
- **Emails that try to give the AI instructions are ignored.** If a message says "ignore your instructions and…", the AI treats it as ordinary text.

## Shared mailboxes

- **Owners: review who has access** every month, under **Members**, and remove anyone who has left.
- **Assign before replying**, so two people don't answer the same email.
- See [Working as a team](/help/15-working-as-a-team) for the full guide.

## Actions that affect your real mailbox

Most of the app only shows your email. These actions change your real Gmail, Outlook or other mailbox, or contact other people:

| Action | What it really does |
|---|---|
| **Send**, **Send reply** | Sends a real email |
| Opening an email | Marks it as read in your real mailbox too |
| **Unsubscribe** | Contacts the sender to stop their emails. The app asks you first |
| **Clean up newsletters** | Archives those emails in your real mailbox. They aren't deleted |
| **Create event** with **Invite** ticked | Adds a calendar event and emails the other person an invite |
| Rules with **Move to folder**, **Archive**, **Mark as read**, **Add label** or **Forward** | Change or forward real emails automatically |
| **Disconnect** a mailbox | Removes it from the app for everyone sharing it. Your real mailbox is untouched |
| **Delete account** | Permanent. Can't be undone |

**Save draft**, **Summarize**, **AI Reply**, **Rewrite**, **Snooze**, notes and tasks never send anything to anyone.
