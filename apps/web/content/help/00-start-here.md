# Start here

New to the app? Read this page first. It takes about five minutes and covers everything you need on your first day.

## What this app does for you

It brings your email into one place and uses AI to save you time:

- **Sorts your inbox** so the emails waiting on an answer from you stand out from receipts, newsletters and "thanks!" messages.
- **Writes reply drafts** for you to check, change and send.
- **Summarises long conversations** in a paragraph.
- **Spots tasks and promises** in your email ("I'll send it by Friday") so they don't get forgotten.
- **Answers questions** about your email ("When is my flight?").
- **Lets a team share one mailbox**, such as `support@yourcompany.com`, without sharing a password.

## The one thing to know about safety

**The AI never sends an email on its own unless you set it up to.**

Out of the box, every reply the AI writes waits for you as a draft. Nothing goes out until you click **Send**. Automatic replies only happen if the account's owner turns on a rule that allows them. See [Safe use guidelines](/help/16-safe-use-guidelines) before you do that.

## Your first 15 minutes

Work through these in order. Each step links to the full instructions.

1. **Connect your mailbox.** Go to **Settings → Accounts** and click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Outlook**. You sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own page, so the app never sees your password. → [Getting started](/help/01-getting-started)
2. **Set your name and timezone.** Go to **Settings → Profile**, fill in your name, job title and company, and click **Use this device's** next to Timezone. Then click **Save**. The AI signs replies with your name, and your timezone decides when reminders and meeting times happen. → [Profile](/help/09-settings-profile)
3. **Wait for your mail to arrive.** The first sync runs in the background and can take a few minutes for a big mailbox. You can keep using the app meanwhile.
4. **Look at Needs reply.** Open **Inbox** and click the **Needs reply** chip. These are the emails where someone is waiting on you. → [Inbox](/help/02-inbox)
5. **Try an AI reply.** Open one of those emails and click **AI Reply** under it. Read the draft, change anything that's wrong, and only then click **Send**. → [AI Reply](/help/02-inbox#ai-reply)
6. **Connect your calendar** *(optional)*. Go to **Settings → Calendars**. This lets the app offer meeting times that don't clash with your diary. → [Calendars](/help/08-settings-calendars)
7. **Choose your notifications.** Go to **Settings → Profile → Notifications** and pick what you want to hear about and where (in the app, by email or in Slack), and the hour your daily digest arrives. → [Notifications](/help/06-notifications)

## Getting around

**On a computer:** the menu on the left lists every page. **Compose** at the top writes a new email. The search box at the top of every page searches your email; its **Ask AI** button asks a question about it instead.

**On a phone:** tap the **☰** button at the top left to open the menu, and tap outside it to close it. When you open an email, tap the **‹** arrow at the top left to go back to your inbox. Scroll down past the messages to reach the reply box.

**The bell** at the top right shows notifications. A red number means you have unread ones.

## The pages, in one line each

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Read, reply, summarise, snooze and share emails |
| **Ask** | Ask a question and get an answer from your own email |
| **Waiting** | Emails you sent that nobody has answered yet |
| **Tasks** | To-dos and meeting requests the AI found in your email |
| **Approvals** | AI replies waiting for your sign-off, if your team uses approvals |
| **Suggestions** | Fixes the app proposes when its AI drafts keep getting rejected |
| **Insights** | How quickly emails get answered, and what the AI did |
| **Documents** | Files the AI can use for facts when it writes replies |
| **Help** | These pages |
| **Settings** | Your mailboxes, calendars, profile, password and devices |
