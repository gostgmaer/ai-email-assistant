# Inbox

## What it is

All your email in one place, from every mailbox you've connected. The AI sorts it, writes reply drafts for you to check, and summarises long conversations. You can also leave notes for your team and snooze emails until later.

## Where to find it

**Inbox**, at the top of the menu on the left. It's also the page you see after signing in.

- **On a computer:** the list of emails is on the left, and the email you open is on the right.
- **On a phone:** you see the list first. Tap an email to open it, and tap **‹** at the top left to go back.

---

## Reading and filtering mail

**Folders:** the tabs at the top of the list are **Inbox**, **Sent**, **Drafts**, **Archive**, **Spam**, **Trash** and **Snoozed**. Click one to switch.

**Priority:** the **Any priority** dropdown shows only **Urgent**, **High**, **Medium** or **Low** emails, as judged by the AI.

**Search:** type a word into **Search email…** at the top of the page and press **Enter**.
- It finds conversations where your words appear in the **subject**, the **sender's name or address**, or the **text of any email** in them. Capital letters don't matter, so `priya`, `Priya` and `harbor.example.net` all work.
- It looks for your words exactly as typed, next to each other: `invoice 1042` finds "invoice 1042" but not "1042 invoice".
- Results start in **Inbox**. To search **Sent**, **Archive** or another folder, click its tab after searching. The search stays applied.
- To clear the search, empty the box and press **Enter**.
- **Can't find it?** Try fewer words, or one unusual word. Or use **Ask AI** next to the box, which searches by meaning, so *"the email about moving office"* can find one that says "relocation". See [Ask your inbox](/help/12-ask-your-inbox).

**Example:** click **Drafts** to see every draft waiting to be checked or finished, from all your mailboxes.

## Needs reply, FYI, Notifications and Newsletters

**What it is:** your Inbox is sorted into groups, so the emails that need you stand out.

**Where:** the chips under the folder tabs. They only appear on the **Inbox** tab. On a narrow screen, swipe the row sideways to see them all.

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **All** | Everything in the Inbox |
| **Needs reply** | Someone is waiting on an answer from you |
| **FYI** | Worth reading, but no answer expected: mail you were only copied on, a "thanks", or a conversation where you sent the last message |
| **Notifications** | Automatic mail such as receipts and account alerts |
| **Newsletters** | Mailing lists and marketing, only if [Keep newsletters](/help/07-settings-accounts#keep-newsletters) is on |

**What happens:** an email moves between groups as new messages arrive. Sorting only happens in the app. It never moves anything in your real mailbox.

> **Tip:** start each day with **Needs reply**. That's your real to-do list.

![The Inbox with the All, Needs reply, FYI and Notifications chips above a list of threads](/help-images/inbox-groups.png)

## Opening an email

**How:** click any email in the list.

**What you see:** every message in the conversation, oldest first, with who sent it, who it went to and when.

**Good to know:**
- Opening an email marks it as read, in the app **and** in your real mailbox.
- **Attachments** appear under a message with a 📎, a name and a size. Click one to download it.
- A grey **No reply needed** label means the AI decided the message doesn't need an answer, such as a "thank you" or mail you were only copied on. It gives the reason. You can still reply yourself.
- A **Grounded in your documents** label means an AI reply used one of your [Documents](/help/05-documents) for facts. Point at it to see which.
- A **Calendar-aware** label means the AI looked at how busy your calendar is while writing. See [Calendar-aware replies](/help/07-settings-accounts#calendar-aware-replies).
- **On a phone**, the whole conversation scrolls as one page. Scroll down past the messages to reach the reply box. The buttons at the top stay in place.

## AI drafts waiting for your review

**What it is:** when a new email arrives that needs an answer, the AI usually writes a reply straight away and leaves it under the message, in a box headed **AI draft reply — awaiting your review**. Nothing is sent until you choose.

**How to use it:**
1. Read the draft. Follow the [checklist before sending](/help/16-safe-use-guidelines#before-you-send-any-ai-written-email).
2. Choose:
   - **Send reply**: sends it as it is.
   - **Edit**: opens it in the full editor so you can change it first.
   - **Discard**: deletes it. See [Discarding an AI draft](#discarding-an-ai-draft).

> **Watch for the amber box.** If a draft shows **Check before sending**, the AI thinks it may say things the conversation doesn't support, such as a price nobody mentioned. Check each listed point before you send.

If the box says the draft is **gated by an approval chain**, it can only be sent after the named people approve it. See [Approvals](/help/11-approvals).

## AI Reply

**What it is:** writes a complete reply to the conversation on demand, for example on an email that doesn't have a draft yet. It uses your [writing style](/help/07-settings-accounts#writing-style), your [Documents](/help/05-documents) where they're relevant, and the name and job title from your [Profile](/help/09-settings-profile).

**Where:** the reply box at the bottom of an open email.

**How to use it:**
1. Open the email.
2. Click **AI Reply** and wait a few seconds. The reply box fills with a draft.
3. Read it and change anything that's wrong.
4. *(optional)* Use the **Rewrite…** dropdown to change what's in the box: **Improve writing**, **Make shorter**, **More formal**, **Friendlier** or **Fix grammar only**.
5. *(optional)* **+ Attach files**, or set **Remind me if no reply in** so you get a reminder if they don't answer.
6. Click **Send**.

**Example:** on an email asking *"Could you send the renewal quote by Friday?"*, **AI Reply** might write:

> *"Hello Tom, thanks for getting in touch. I'll send the renewal quote for 2027 before Friday. Best regards, Alex"*

Before sending, check it's true: will you really send it by Friday?

**What happens:** clicking **AI Reply** or **Rewrite** only fills the box. It's always safe to try. **Send** sends a real email, and it can't be undone.

## Summarize

**What it is:** a short AI summary of the whole conversation, with the key points. Useful for long back-and-forth emails, or when you pick up a conversation from a colleague.

**Where:** the **Summarize** button at the top of an open email.

**How:** click it. A blue **AI Summary** box appears above the messages within a few seconds.

**Example:** *"Dana Lee says invoice 1042 lists 40 hours, but 36 were agreed, and asks you to check it."*

**What happens:** the summary is saved with the conversation. Everyone who has access to that mailbox sees it, and it also appears in the Inbox list. It isn't updated by itself when new messages arrive: click **Refresh summary** to bring it up to date.

## Notes (internal, team-only)

**What it is:** comments on an email that are **never sent**. Only people who share this mailbox can see them (see [Working as a team](/help/15-working-as-a-team)). Useful for "I've called them" or "waiting for finance".

**Where:** the **Notes** button at the top of an open email. The number in brackets is how many notes there are.

**How to use it:**
1. Click **Notes**.
2. Type in **Add a note for your team…**.
3. Click **Add**.
4. To remove a note, click **Delete** next to it.

**Example:** *"Called them, they'd prefer Tuesday. Waiting on their reply before we reschedule."*

**What happens:** the note is saved on the conversation with your name and the time. The person you're emailing never sees it.

## Snooze

**What it is:** hides an email until later, so it doesn't clutter today's Inbox but isn't forgotten.

**Where:** the **Snooze…** dropdown at the top of an open email.

**How:** pick **Later today (+3h)**, **Tomorrow morning** or **Next week**. The email disappears from your Inbox and you go back to the list.

**When the time comes:** it comes back to your Inbox by itself, and you get a notification. On a shared mailbox, the person it's assigned to gets it.

**To bring it back early:** open the **Snoozed** tab, open the email and click **Snoozed — unsnooze**.

**Good to know:** snoozing only happens in the app. Your real mailbox isn't changed.

## Assigning a thread (Shared Inbox only)

**What it is:** marks which teammate is handling an email. It only appears on mailboxes shared with two or more people. See [Working as a team](/help/15-working-as-a-team).

**Where:** a dropdown at the top of an open email, showing the current person or **Unassigned**. It's hidden on phones, so use a computer.

**How:** pick a name, or **Unassigned** to clear it.

**What happens:** everyone with access to the mailbox sees who's handling it straight away. If that person is later removed from the mailbox, the email goes back to **Unassigned**.

## Meeting requests: Propose times

**What it is:** when an email asks to meet, the app can offer free times from your calendar, then book the one the other person picks. You need a connected [calendar](/help/08-settings-calendars).

**Where:** a blue **Meeting request** card at the top of the email.

**How to use it:**
1. Click **Propose times**. Free times are written into the reply box. Nothing is sent or booked yet.
2. Change the text if you like, then click **Send**.
3. When they answer, the card marks the time they chose as **Their pick**, usually within a few minutes.
4. Check the time and the title, then click **Create event**.

If the app didn't recognise their answer, pick the time on the card yourself. **Propose new times** starts again with fresh times.

![A Meeting request card with three times, the 2:00 PM one marked Their pick, an event title, the Invite option and a Create event button](/help-images/meeting-card.png)

**Example:** Priya writes *"Can we talk Tuesday afternoon? I'm in Berlin."* **Propose times** writes:

> Here are a few times that work for me:
>
> - Tuesday, Nov 3, 12:00 PM GMT+5:30 (Tue, 7:30 AM GMT+1)
> - Tuesday, Nov 3, 2:00 PM GMT+5:30 (Tue, 9:30 AM GMT+1)
> - Tuesday, Nov 3, 4:00 PM GMT+5:30 (Tue, 11:30 AM GMT+1)
>
> Let me know which suits you best.

**What happens:**
- **Which times:** only free time on your calendar, on weekdays between 9:00 and 17:00 your time, up to two weeks ahead. Where possible it follows the day and time they asked for.
- **Time zones:** yours comes from your [Profile](/help/09-settings-profile). Theirs appears in brackets only if their email mentions where they are. If the times look wrong, check your timezone in Profile.
- **Create event:** puts a real event in your calendar. With **Invite** ticked, the other person also gets a calendar invitation by email and a short confirmation reply in this conversation. Untick **Invite** to only block the time in your own calendar.

## Unsubscribe

**Where:** a newsletter shows an **Unsubscribe…** button above its messages.

**How:** click **Unsubscribe…**, then choose one of the ways the sender offers:
- **Unsubscribe in one click**, then **Yes, unsubscribe**: the app does it for you.
- **Open the sender's unsubscribe page**: opens their website, where you finish it.
- **Email an unsubscribe request**: opens a new email with the request filled in. Nothing is sent until you click **Send**.

**What happens:** this really contacts the sender for you, so it always asks first. Some senders take a few days to stop. If a sender offers no way to unsubscribe, the app tells you.

## Clean up newsletters

**Where:** the **Newsletters** group in the Inbox.

**How:** under **Clean up newsletters older than**, choose an age. The app shows how many emails that matches. Click **Archive N** to go ahead, or **Cancel**.

**What happens:** those emails are moved to the archive in your real mailbox. They're not deleted, and you can still find them there.

## AI drafts that send by themselves (Cancel send)

This only happens if the mailbox's owner has turned on automatic replies. See [Safe use guidelines](/help/16-safe-use-guidelines#turning-on-automatic-replies-owners).

**What it is:** some automatic replies wait a short time before they're sent: during the [undo window](/help/07-settings-accounts#automation-settings), during quiet hours, or because a rule delays them. While they wait, they sit under the message they answer.

**Where:** the box under the message reads **AI reply — sending automatically** and *"Sends at <time> unless you cancel."*

**How:** click **Cancel send** to keep it as an ordinary draft for you to check. Editing it, sending it yourself or discarding it also stops the automatic send. It can never go out twice.

**What happens:** if you do nothing, it's sent at the time shown. If automatic sending is switched off for the mailbox before then, it isn't sent and you get a notification instead.

## Discarding an AI draft

**How:** click **Discard** on an AI draft, then say why: **Wrong facts**, **Wrong tone**, **Shouldn't reply**, **Too long** or **Other**. **Just discard** skips the question, and **Keep it** keeps the draft.

![An AI draft after clicking Discard, showing the reason chips, Just discard and Keep it](/help-images/discard-reasons.png)

**What happens:** the draft is deleted. Your reason helps the app spot patterns and suggest fixes on the [Suggestions](/help/13-suggestions) page, so it's worth the extra click.
