# Compose

## What it is

Writing a new email. To answer an email you received, use the reply box under it instead; see [Inbox](/help/02-inbox).

## Where to find it

The **Compose** button at the top of the menu on the left. On a phone, tap **☰** at the top left first.

## How to use it

1. **From**: if you have more than one mailbox, choose which one to send from. Your **Primary** mailbox is chosen for you.
2. **To**: one or more email addresses, separated by commas.
3. **Cc** / **Bcc** *(optional)*: people to copy. People in **Bcc** are hidden from everyone else.
4. **Subject**.
5. **Message**: write your email. The **Rewrite with AI** dropdown polishes what you've written: **Improve writing**, **Make shorter**, **More formal**, **Friendlier** or **Fix grammar only**.
6. *(optional)* **+ Attach files**: up to 10 files, 3 MB in total. Remove a file with its **✕**. For anything bigger, share a link from Google Drive, OneDrive or similar instead.
7. *(optional)* **Remind me if no reply in**: pick 1 to 14 days. If nobody answers by then, you get a reminder task and a notification.
8. Click one of:
   - **Save draft**: keeps it without sending. You'll find it in **Inbox → Drafts**.
   - **Send**: sends it now.

> **Check before you click Send:** the addresses, the subject, and anything the AI rewrote. A sent email can't be called back.

## Example

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| From | `you@example.com` |
| To | `someone@example.com` |
| Subject | `Quick follow-up` |
| Message | `Hi, just checking in on the proposal. Any update?` |

Not sure about the wording yet? Click **Save draft**, and come back to it later from **Drafts**.

## To, Cc or Bcc?

| Field | Use it for | Who can see the address |
|---|---|---|
| **To** | The people you're writing to and expect an answer from | Everyone on the email |
| **Cc** | People who should know, but needn't act | Everyone on the email |
| **Bcc** | People who should get a copy without the others knowing, or a big group whose addresses should stay private | Nobody else |

**Example:** you send a proposal **To** the client, **Cc** your manager so they're in the loop, and **Bcc** nobody.

> **Writing to a big group of people who don't know each other?** Put them in **Bcc**, so you don't share everyone's address with everyone else.

## Getting the AI to help

- **Polish what you wrote:** type a rough version, then pick from **Rewrite with AI**. For example, *"hey can u send the invoice asap thx"* with **Improve writing** becomes *"Could you please send the invoice as soon as possible? Thanks."*
- **Not happy with the rewrite?** Edit it by hand, or pick another option. Rewriting only changes the text in the box. Nothing is sent.
- **Writing in your own style:** if the mailbox's owner has built a [writing style](/help/07-settings-accounts#writing-style), rewrites follow your usual greeting, sign-off and tone.

## Editing a draft

Open it from **Inbox → Drafts**. It opens in this same form, filled in, with an extra button:

- **Discard draft**: deletes the draft straight away, without asking. This can't be undone.
- **Save draft** and **Send** work as above.

Attachments and reminders can't be added to a saved draft yet. Add them when you write and send in one go.

**Waiting for approval?** If a rule requires sign-off for this reply, a banner at the top explains what's happening:
- **Amber, awaiting approval:** shows which step it's on and who's next. **Send** stays switched off until everyone has approved; see [Approvals](/help/11-approvals).
- **Red, rejected:** someone sent it back for changes. Edit it, and you can then send it yourself.
- **Expired or cancelled:** nobody finished approving it in time, or the approver was removed from the mailbox. Check it and send it yourself if it's ready.

## What happens

- **Save draft** keeps the draft in this app only. It doesn't appear in your Gmail or Outlook drafts.
- **Send** sends a real email from the mailbox you chose. There's no undo.
- Replying from an email offers the same **+ Attach files** and **Remind me if no reply in** options under the reply box.
